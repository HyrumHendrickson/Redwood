# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers are expected to engage respectfully and professionally. Disagreements should address ideas and evidence, not people.

Expected behaviour:

- be courteous and constructive in issues, reviews and discussions
- accept good-faith criticism of work and give it in the same spirit
- assume competence and good intent until shown otherwise

Unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment, threats, insults or discriminatory remarks
- publishing another person's private information without permission
- sustained disruption of discussions or deliberately misleading contributions

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this code, and may restrict participation by repeat or serious offenders.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through a GitHub private message or the contact options on the maintainer's profile at https://github.com/HyrumHendrickson. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, and when an individual is representing the project in public.
